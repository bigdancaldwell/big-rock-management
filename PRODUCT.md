## Platform

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS (plus a small amount of vanilla JS), hosted on the owner's Hostinger Business plan alongside Big Rock Arms (bigrockarms.com), deployed by Hostinger's Git integration from the `main` branch of the `bigdancaldwell/big-rock-management` GitHub repo. Domain bigrockco.com (DNS at HostGator: A @ → 82.29.157.35, CNAME www → bigrockco.com). Quote form submissions go through a free form service (Formspree or similar) to BigRockLMTD@gmail.com. The owner needs to create the form endpoint and paste it into `main.js` (FORM_ENDPOINT). Until then, the form opens the visitor's email app with the request filled in.

## Users

- **Property owners in the Tucson metro area** with a home, casita, or investment property they want to run as a short-term rental (Airbnb/VRBO) without managing it themselves.
- **Homeowners, landlords, and STR hosts** who need a deep clean or a full reset (move-out, post-guest, pre-listing).
- **Anyone with a pile to get rid of**: junk and furniture, yard and construction debris, or a whole estate or move-out cleanout.
- **Buyers and sellers** who arrive here and need to be routed to Big Rock Realty Group.

## Product Purpose

bigrockco.com is the umbrella site for the Big Rock group of companies. It explains each service in plain terms, routes each visitor to the right service, and turns interest into a quote request by email. Success means a visitor finds their service within seconds and sends a quote request.

## Positioning

One locally owned Tucson team covers a property from end to end: running it as a rental, cleaning and resetting it, hauling out what's left, and (through Big Rock Realty Group) buying or selling it. A single-service competitor can't honestly claim that.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Short-term rental management** (Big Rock Management): listing setup and pricing (Airbnb/VRBO setup, photos, dynamic pricing); guest communication (messaging, check-in, reviews); turnovers and upkeep (cleans between stays, restocking, coordinating maintenance). Permit and tax filing help is NOT offered.
- **Property cleaning**: operates as **Big Rock Service Solutions**, a sub-brand. Deep cleans and resets.
- **Hauling & bulk debris removal** (Big Rock Management): junk and furniture; yard and construction debris; estate and move-out cleanouts. Commercial hauling is NOT offered.
- **Real estate**: Big Rock Realty Group, the group's main business, is live at https://bigrockaz.com, and that site carries the team's full compliance. The realty page routes buyers and sellers there. This site only needs the brokerage name (Keller Williams Southern Arizona) plus the Equal Housing statement. The owner confirmed these are sufficient (2026-10-04).
- **Licensing**: the owner confirmed short-term rental management does not need to run under the brokerage (2026-10-04).
- **Service area**: Tucson metro (Tucson, Oro Valley, Marana, Vail, Sahuarita).
- **Contact**: email only, BigRockLMTD@gmail.com. No named person, no phone number.

## Product Principles

1. Route people fast: every visitor should see their service within one glance.
2. Make the one-team story concrete: show how the services connect around a single property.
3. Claim only what's true; show the work instead of adding adjectives.
4. Sending an email for a quote should take as little effort as possible.
