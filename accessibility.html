Accessibility

Effective October 4, 2026

We want everyone to be able to use this site and reach us, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, magnification, or other assistive technology.

Our standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA.

What we've done

  • Text and controls meet AA color-contrast levels
  • Every page works with a keyboard, with visible focus outlines and a skip-to-content link
  • Images have text descriptions, and decorative artwork is hidden from screen readers
  • Form fields have labels, and errors are explained in text, not just color
  • Animation is reduced for anyone who turns on "reduce motion"
  • Layouts adapt to phones and to zoom up to 200%

Need help or found a problem?

If anything on this site is hard to use, email BigRockLMTD@gmail.com with the page and the problem. We'll fix it, and we'll help you get the information or the quote you need another way in the meantime.
